How Margins works

  1. Select text in any app. Tap Margins in the menu that pops up, or tap Share and pick Margins.
  2. It's saved at once. A card lets you add a note, tags and the source. Close it and the highlight stays saved.
  3. From a browser, the page title, author and link are filled in. From Kindle and other book apps, the book and author are read from what the app shares.
  4. If an app sends only the text (PDF readers), choose the source from your recent ones. Your next highlights from that app within 3 hours go to the same source automatically.
  5. For a daily highlight on your home screen: long-press the home screen → Widgets → Margins. Tap ↻ on it for another one.
  6. Use ⋮ → Export for Excel or Markdown at any time.

Everything is stored only on this phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no server. The app connects to the internet only to read a web page's title and author. Make a backup before uninstalling, because uninstalling deletes the app's data.
